One reading for everything your system does.

Sign in once with your CSNet Manager account. The integration discovers the installation and creates entities for whatever your unit reports. Nothing to map by hand, nothing to flash.

Full sensor reference
Entities created by the integration
SpotPlatformWhat you get
Sp1 climate A thermostat per zone. C1 and C2, air or water circuits. Off, heat, cool and heat/cool, comfort and eco presets, silent mode, fan speed on fan-coil systems.
Sp2 water_heater Domestic hot water. Off, eco and performance (boost), with the tank temperature as its own sensor.
Sp3 water_heater Swimming pool. On or off, target between 24 and 33 °C.
Sp4 sensor The water loop. Water in and out, flow, pressure, pump speed, mixing valve, outdoor and mean outdoor temperature.
Sp5 sensor Inside the outdoor unit. Compressor frequency and current, discharge and suction pressure, expansion valve, fan speed, defrost. The secondary cycle too, on units that report it.
Sp6 sensor Energy and efficiency. Instant consumption, heating and cooling thermal power, instant COP, daily consumption and daily COP for space heating and hot water.
Sp7 number Fixed water temperature. Heating and cooling setpoints per water circuit, when it runs without outdoor compensation.
Sp8 sensor Alarms you actually see. Code, origin and description, a persistent notification when one fires, a filter for codes you choose to ignore, and a service to clear them.
Sp9 sensor Link health. Wi-Fi signal of the ATW-IOT-01, connectivity and last communication, so you know when the cloud is the problem.

Unofficial, by necessity.

Hitachi publishes no API for the ATW-IOT-01 and offers no Home Assistant integration. The author contacted Hitachi several times asking for one, or at least for documentation. None of those messages got an answer.

So the integration does what your browser does. It signs in to CSNet Manager with your account and reads the same data the web app shows, then sends your changes back the same way the web app does.

It is not affiliated with, endorsed or supported by Hitachi. When Hitachi changes its website, the integration can stop working until it is updated.

Polling
Every 60 seconds by default. Configurable, but please keep it at 60 s or more.
Traffic
3 HTTPS requests per poll. Sign-in and alarm translations on start-up.
Destination
www.csnetmanager.com only. No telemetry, no analytics, no third-party service.
Credentials
Stored in your Home Assistant config entry. Sent only to CSNet Manager.

Running in three steps.

  1. Install from HACS

    Open the repository in HACS and download it. Or search for Hitachi CSNet Home in HACS.

    Open in HACS

  2. Restart Home Assistant

    Settings, System, Restart. Home Assistant only loads new integrations at start-up.

  3. Add the integration

    Sign in with the username and password you use on csnetmanager.com. Zones appear a few seconds later.

    Add integration

You need an ATW-IOT-01 module on the unit, a working CSNet Manager account, and Home Assistant with HACS. No HACS? Install manually.
